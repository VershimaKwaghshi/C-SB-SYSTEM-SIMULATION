The core idea

Loss recovery without new depositsWhen a trader loses half an account, the usual answer is simple. The trader carries the loss alone, keeps trading what is left and tries to win it back, or adds new money.

C&SB changes that answer. The loss becomes a recovery case, and all of the responsible manager's eligible earnings inside the network go to paying it back at once. A Social Bond can let trading continue while that happens.

Everything else in C&SB is a feature that serves this one purpose.Why it matters. A trader does not lose the whole relationship because one account failed.

If the trader stops paying

Before the account is issued, the money paid is refunded less the service fee. If the account was issued early, there is no standard refund and the account is recovered or ended. The Accounts tab lets you test this.
Accounts and managers
A person manages only one account personally. Every extra account goes to a different manager. That is how one purchase creates work for other traders.

How a profit is shared

The trading platform shows the full profit. The manager sees only the manager share as the amount available to withdraw.
Manager recovery
When an account falls 50 percent, the responsible manager is asked to pay it back from earnings the manager already receives. Not from new deposits.
Social Bond (SB)
Recovery takes time. The Social Bond lets the trader keep trading meanwhile. A licensed provider supplies the credit. C&SB only connects everyone and applies the rules.
Conflict rule
One person may work with two accounts. The same currency pair must never be open on both at the same time. This keeps the extra accounts from becoming one trader's private book.

Why a profit gets nothingIf a conflicting trade made money, the manager still gets no share. Otherwise someone could break the rule on purpose and keep the upside. A loss, on the other hand, starts recovery at once.The rule is checked on the server using the broker's own position data. The screen only shows it.
